<GoExampleRow docs={"If you just wanted to wait, you could have used `time.Sleep`. One reason a timer may be useful is that you can cancel the timer before it fires. Here's an example of that."} language="go" code={"    timer2 := time.NewTimer(time.Second)\n    go func() {\n        <-timer2.C\n        fmt.Println(\"Timer 2 fired\")\n    }()\n    stop2 := timer2.Stop()\n    if stop2 {\n        fmt.Println(\"Timer 2 stopped\")\n    }"} />
